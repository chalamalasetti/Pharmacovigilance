How matches work

Product notices and research match confirmed product records. Regulatory notices match jurisdictions and require human triage. Trial updates match an organization’s recorded role.

These are review prompts, not approved legal interpretations.

Evidence window: up to 365 days of available product and research matches; latest captured regulatory source per document; up to 200 trials with a source update in the last 30 days. View the company portfolio for full available trial coverage.
