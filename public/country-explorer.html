Loaded study records · phases can overlap

Study location is registry-reported. Overall recruitment status is not local site status. Trial medicines can include comparators and placebo; sponsor roles do not establish ownership or approval.

Read the evidence correctly

Counts represent source records, with possible overlap between registries. An authorisation, an NDC listing and a marketed status describe different things. Company roles are source-reported relationships, not verified global ownership. Missing data does not establish that no products exist.
