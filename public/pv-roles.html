Jurisdiction and legal-role boundaries

This directory does not require every company to employ separate people for each title. Functions may be combined or outsourced. MAH/sponsor responsibilities and statutory roles require source-specific review. The EU QPPV qualification model is not a universal MD/PharmD plus years-of-experience rule; a medically trained assistant may be required. UK QPPV and national-contact arrangements differ. Do not infer legal appointments or permissions from a selected title.
