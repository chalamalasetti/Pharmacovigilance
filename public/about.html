About PV Info

PV Info gathers drug safety and regulatory news for pharmacovigilance, drug safety and regulatory affairs teams into one page you can filter by region, country and topic.

Where the updates come from

  • Regulator feeds, marked Official: FDA (MedWatch safety alerts, drug and biologics updates, press releases, recalls), EMA news, MHRA Drug Safety Update and alerts, Health Canada recalls and safety alerts, TGA safety alerts and WHO news.
  • News searches for every other agency in the directory (PMDA, CDSCO, NMPA, MFDS, ANVISA, Swissmedic, SAHPRA, SFDA and more), and for PV topics such as safety signals, boxed warnings, PSURs, GVP, ICH E2 and nitrosamines. These are kept only when they mention medicines, safety or regulation.

Updates are refreshed every hour and kept for 60 days. Each one is placed in a country (official feeds by their regulator, news by the agencies and countries it names) and in a topic by plain keyword rules: safety alerts and signals, recalls and quality, label changes, guidelines, regulatory changes, policy, inspections and enforcement, approvals, or general industry news.

Follow updates elsewhere

Subscribe to the RSS feed in Outlook, Teams, Slack or any feed reader. Any filtered view can be bookmarked or shared: the filters are part of the address.

Please note

PV Info is a reading aid, not a regulatory record. Topic and country tags are automatic and can be wrong, and some regulators publish only in their own language or without a feed. Always confirm details with the official source before acting.
