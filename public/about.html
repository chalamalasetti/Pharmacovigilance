About PV Info

PV Info gathers drug safety and regulatory news for pharmacovigilance, drug safety and regulatory affairs teams into one page you can filter by region, country and topic.

Where the updates come from

  • Regulator feeds, marked Official: FDA (MedWatch safety alerts, drug and biologics updates, press releases, recalls), EMA news, MHRA Drug Safety Update and alerts, Health Canada recalls and safety alerts, TGA safety alerts and WHO news.
  • News searches for every other agency in the directory (PMDA, CDSCO, NMPA, MFDS, ANVISA, Swissmedic, SAHPRA, SFDA and more), and for PV topics such as safety signals, boxed warnings, PSURs, GVP, ICH E2 and nitrosamines. These are kept only when they mention medicines, safety or regulation.

Updates are refreshed every hour and kept for 60 days. Each one is placed in a country (official feeds by their regulator, news by the agencies and countries it names) and in a topic by plain keyword rules: safety alerts and signals, recalls and quality, label changes, guidelines, regulatory changes, policy, inspections and enforcement, approvals, or general industry news.

Ask PV Info and the PV Globe

The chat bubble in the corner answers questions such as “FDA safety alerts this week”, “PMDA label changes” or “nitrosamine recalls” with the matching updates, each linked to its source. It searches this site's updates only and is not an AI, so it never invents an answer.

The PV Globe is a spinning 3D map of the same updates: countries glow by how many updates they have, red pulses mark a safety alert or recall in the last 7 days, and the EU and international bodies (WHO, ICH, CIOMS, UMC) have markers of their own. Tap a country to see its latest updates.

My PV Briefing and impact cards

My PV Briefing is a short daily view built from your role, countries, topics and watchlist. Every update opens into an impact card: what changed (quoted from the source), who may be affected, potential business and technical impact, the dates the source states (publication, effective date, consultation deadline, transition period), and the evidence behind it. You can save, follow, assess, assign or dismiss an update with a reason.

The briefing has views for case processing and operations, safety scientists and physicians, QPPV and compliance, PV systems and configuration, and PV leaders (a weekly summary with pending assessments and coverage gaps). Switch views at any time, rename them under Groups, or add groups of your own.

Impact cards are produced by keyword rules, not AI. Each “potential” implication shows the words that triggered it and is a prompt to check, not a conclusion. Your profile and actions are stored only in this browser; use Export to move them elsewhere.

Follow updates elsewhere

Subscribe to the RSS feed in Outlook, Teams, Slack or any feed reader. Any filtered view can be bookmarked or shared: the filters are part of the address.

Please note

PV Info is a reading aid, not a regulatory record. Topic and country tags are automatic and can be wrong, and some regulators publish only in their own language or without a feed. Always confirm details with the official source before acting.
