# Drug-safety baseline inventory and impact-assessment worksheet

**Baseline inventory—scope-dependent; validation required. Partial research draft, not an approved or complete legal baseline.**

## 1. Executive summary

Research checked 1 November 2026. Intended jurisdictions: US, EU, UK, Canada and Japan. Initial evidence supports selected US, EU-network technical, UK-guidance and Canadian provisions. Japan operative requirements are not verified. Human medicines across clinical development and post-market use are the intended scope; actual product/authorisation applicability remains unresolved. No company product details were supplied. No requirement below is automatically approved.

Clinical reporting is distinct from post-market reporting. Different clock starts, case categories and recipients must not be flattened into a global deadline. EMA operates within an EU/national network; it is not a substitute for Member-State legal analysis. MHRA guidance and GB/NI interactions require separate review. Japanese originals prevail over informational English translations according to the PMDA translation notice. Medical devices, veterinary products, cosmetics, supplements and full combination-product coverage are excluded.

## 7. Transparent risk scoring (proposed company control, not a regulatory rule)

Keep the six dimensions visible; do not sum them into one compliance score.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient severity | No credible harm identified | Potential reversible harm | Potential death, serious harm or critical safety decision failure |
| Compliance severity | Administrative discrepancy | Material control/submission weakness | Missed mandatory obligation or critical regulatory risk |
| Likelihood | Unlikely with evidence | Plausible | Occurring or expected repeatedly |
| Detectability | Automated prevention/detection | Manual routine detection | Unlikely to detect before harm/deadline |
| Complexity | Single controlled update | Several coordinated controls | Cross-system/vendor/market validation |
| Time pressure | Adequate evidenced lead time | Limited contingency | Overdue or deadline/transition imminent |

Escalate patient severity 3 immediately to safety leadership. Escalate compliance severity 3 with imminent/overdue time pressure to regulatory/quality leadership without waiting for a combined score. Complexity is a planning input, not a reduction in urgency. Define actual due dates and local escalation expectations for the change.
