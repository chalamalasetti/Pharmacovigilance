Know what you are reading.

Regulator feed identifies an item from a configured official feed. News coverage identifies a publisher report; mentioning an agency does not make it an official notice.

Impact cards use keyword rules to suggest potential business, process and system implications. They are review prompts. Check the original source and your company’s applicability before acting.
