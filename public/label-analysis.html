01 Define the question

Dates filter label evidence, not product approvals. Products approved before 2000 remain eligible. Registry dates are source dates at application level; they are not necessarily the original approval of every presentation or indication.

02 Build your cohort

Use all captured applications in your market, or import public products and companies. Confirm every imported row before running.

Up to 500 rows / 200 KB. Columns: product, company, market, application. Exact captured brand, ingredient, company name/ID, holder or application matching. A company can map to many applications.

03 Define your terms

Format: phrase | literal, variant or related. Related terms are separate concepts, not interchangeable synonyms. Phrase matches ignore case and punctuation; no stemming or clinical interpretation.

Array of {term,type,code,version}; type PT, LLT or SMQ requires an eight-digit code and version. Replaces the term list. User supplied mappings are not dictionary validated; no official SMQ algorithm runs.

Ingredient-only recommendations cannot be attributed to an exact application and remain unmatched.
