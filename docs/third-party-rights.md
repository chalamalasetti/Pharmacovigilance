# Third-party rights and current restrictions

PV Info has no MedDRA or WHODrug subscription. The hosted app does not supply those dictionaries, automatic dictionary coding or standardized query expansion. Coded term imports and restored coded analysis settings are blocked. CIOMS template filling and coded case XML download are disabled pending documented rights review. Ordinary PDF output is a PV Info training print, not a CIOMS form.

Names of regulators, vendors, dictionaries and standards may identify the referenced source; they do not imply subscription, affiliation, approval or a licence grant. Public medical phrases and phrases appearing in source documents are not presented as licensed dictionary entries. User-defined related phrases are not verified synonyms.

## Materials with separate terms

- MedDRA and WHODrug terminology: no dictionary datasets are authorized for this deployment. Do not add dictionaries, hierarchy files, coded expansions or subscription credentials.
- CIOMS forms: no form artwork is supplied by PV Info and hosted template filling is disabled. A downloaded or uploaded form does not by itself establish permission to adapt or redistribute it.
- ICH/HL7 schemas and validation tooling: retain bundled attribution and notices in `public/e2b-third-party-notices.txt`. Structural examples do not grant rights to licensed terminology or establish regulator acceptance.
- Software and fonts: dependencies and bundled assets retain their individual licences and attribution. Free availability does not mean absence of licence conditions. Review dependency changes and distribution obligations before release.
- Regulator documents and other public sources: record origin, retrieval and applicable source terms. Prefer source links and necessary excerpts; do not assume all agencies permit bulk republication or commercial redistribution.
- General project code: no general source-code licence has yet been selected. Public repository visibility is not a blanket reuse licence.

## Release requirements

Maintain a reviewed inventory of source, owner, licence or permission, intended use, redistribution conditions and retained notices. Unverified rights are a release-review item, not presumed permission. Changes to commercial use, document redistribution, terminology access or form export need a fresh rights review. This document is an operational restriction and inventory starting point, not legal certification.
