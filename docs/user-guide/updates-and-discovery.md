# Updates, search and global discovery

Find updates, understand impact cards and explore the same evidence through country views, the archive and Ask PV Info.

## Find and filter updates
1. Open [Updates](https://www.pvinfo.org/).
2. Enter a medicine, safety topic, agency or phrase in Search. Start broad, then narrow the country/body and topic.
3. Choose Regulators for official feeds, News for secondary reporting, or All for both. An Official badge describes the source classification, not a company-applicability decision.
4. Select Published within. The homepage windows cover recent content; use Archive for older collected records.
5. Use Follow this country and Only countries I follow to focus on followed countries. Following is a reading preference, not a statement that you operate in that market.
6. Select Clear filters if results look unexpectedly empty. Show more reveals another page of matching results.

Filtered views can be represented in the page URL. Share only the public reading link; do not append confidential product plans or patient details to a search URL. Dates without source evidence must not be interpreted as known effective dates.

## Read an update and its impact card
Open the original-source title link to inspect the notice. Impact card expands the app's structured reading prompts: what may have changed, possible business/technical implications, source-stated dates and supporting terms. Classification and potential impacts are produced by rules and may be incomplete or wrong. They are not a legal interpretation or an approved implementation requirement.

Supported save, assess, assign, follow and dismiss actions depend on account state and the current workspace. Add a reason where requested. Saving an item does not create a verified company case automatically. For the full company workflow, confirm products/markets in [Company Briefing](company-briefing.md) and continue through the [Inbox](company-change-inbox.md) and [Action Center](action-center.md).

## Key updates and empty results
Key updates this week prioritizes recent matching items; it is not a guarantee that every important notice was captured. An empty result means no loaded records matched your filters. It does not mean the agency issued no notices or that your company has no obligation. Clear filters, widen the date range, inspect the original regulator site and ask an administrator about missing coverage if necessary.

## Ask PV Info
Open the chat bubble and try an organizational query such as FDA safety alerts this week or nitrosamine recalls. Ask PV Info searches the app's loaded update index and returns source-linked matches. It does not provide a general legal/medical answer or retrieve every document on the web. It is not an administrator support inbox; messages are not guaranteed to reach an administrator. Use the account assistance route for access problems.

## Global view and regulator directory
[Global view](https://www.pvinfo.org/globe.html) presents updates geographically. Select a country or an international-body marker to inspect its recent updates. Map intensity and safety/recall indicators reflect collected records, not country-level risk or completeness.

[Regulators](https://www.pvinfo.org/agencies.html) helps you find agencies and public source links. [Sources we cover](https://www.pvinfo.org/coverage.html) describes configured feed coverage. A country appearing in a directory is not proof that all of its primary documents are ingested, translated or verified.

## Search the Archive
Open [Archive](https://www.pvinfo.org/archive.html), choose search words, country, topic, official/news scope and From/To dates, then Search. Clear resets the criteria and Show more loads additional matching records. The archive contains previously collected updates, not a complete historical regulator archive. Public reading does not require signing in when the service is available.

## Follow RSS
The [RSS feed](https://www.pvinfo.org/feed.xml) provides recent public updates to a compatible reader. It is separate from private company assessment status. RSS can serve the last built copy during a runtime-data outage, so compare publication/retrieval evidence rather than assuming every fetch represents a newly collected item.
