# End-to-end worked examples

Practice the full journey with clearly hypothetical organizational examples.

## Example A: a first reading session
Scenario: you want to review recent UK drug-safety notices.
1. Open Updates, choose the UK/body scope, Regulators and an appropriate publication window.
2. Open a notice and verify the original MHRA source and dates.
3. Expand Impact card. Treat potential implications as prompts and read the evidence that triggered them.
4. Save/follow it in a supported workspace if useful, or continue public reading without creating an account.
5. Use My PV Briefing for a tailored reading view. This does not create a closed company assessment.

Expected result: a traceable source reading and, if saved, a visible item in the intended workspace. No legal/company applicability is inferred automatically.

## Example B: a matched product alert through closure
Scenario: an approved user monitors a hypothetical medicine in a selected market.
1. In Company Explorer, identify the intended company and verify the product/source record.
2. In Company Briefing, select the market and product and confirm scope.
3. In Company Change Inbox, select that company and My private account or the intended team.
4. Open a matched alert's original evidence and why-it-matched explanation.
5. Start assessment or Send for assessment, then open/start the case in Action Center.
6. Record Relevant with a company-specific reason. If uncertain, choose More information needed and keep the case open.
7. For a Relevant decision, assign a supported action type, authorized owner, due date and clear instruction.
8. Draft any necessary implementation requirements using actual current controls and system/process knowledge.
9. Perform the work outside the app as appropriate, then submit what changed and a stable supporting evidence link.
10. For team work, have the designated independent reviewer inspect evidence and approve or return it. For personal work, retain the explicit self-review designation.
11. Export the case when needed and confirm it appears Closed in the correct scope.

Expected result: original evidence, applicability rationale, assigned work, completion evidence and a recorded verification decision. A closed personal case must not be relabeled independently verified.

## Example C: regulatory document to reviewed baseline and company action
Scenario: an official agency revises a guidance document. This is a demonstration workflow, not an assertion about a real regulatory change.
1. An administrator registers/monitors the official document under a verified agency, or reviews a Source Discovery candidate and registers it.
2. Collection captures the permitted original and extracts candidate clauses/automatic drafts. Check capture status; investigate partial extraction.
3. From Source Documents, open Baseline Review for the exact capture.
4. Check each selected draft against the archived original, including definitions, scope, exceptions, tables and cross-references. Save corrections, submit for review and publish only after the required human review/confirmation.
5. For an existing obligation, revise the existing stable requirement in Requirements Library instead of creating a duplicate ID.
6. In Regulatory Change Review, compare previous/current source evidence, record human triage and link affected approved requirement versions. If information is incomplete, keep the uncertainty explicit.
7. Choose a confirmed company portfolio and private/team destination, then Open private assessment when triage permits.
8. In Action Center, document the company's applicability and current control. Draft the supported business/system/process/data/training requirements relevant to the actual organization.
9. Assign implementation work, submit evidence and complete the applicable reviewer verification.
10. Review the exported case package and pinned source/requirement/triage versions. The newer public source must not silently replace the record of what your case used.

Expected result: a chain from captured source to reviewed interpretation, company decision, implementation evidence and closure. Source publication, library approval and company approval remain distinct decisions.

## Example D: more information needed or no action
Choose More information needed when source scope, dates or company applicability cannot be established. Record the question and keep assessment open. Choose Not relevant only with a reason supported by the selected company/product/market context. Use the no-action completion/evidence and verification route offered by Action Center; do not remove the case merely because it does not apply.

## Evidence to check during a pilot
Use one source document and one company scope. Check navigation, permissions, source citations, versions, saved rationale, owner/due date, implementation-draft review, evidence links, reviewer access, closed status and export. Record any gap rather than marking the workflow complete because the screens render. Organization-specific qualification/validation is a separate activity from this functional walkthrough.
