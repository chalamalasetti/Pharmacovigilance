# Label Intelligence

## Available evidence

Explore captured public-source label evidence and available US approval and DailyMed version journeys. Source publication, SPL effective and approval dates have distinct meanings. Historical coverage is limited and must be reviewed.

## Free-text label analysis

Label analysis supports ordinary literal phrases and user-defined related phrases only. Related phrases are not verified synonyms or standardized queries. PV Info has no MedDRA or WHODrug subscription. Licensed terminology imports and restored PT/LLT/SMQ metadata are blocked. No automatic dictionary coding, SMQ expansion or licensed dictionary validation is supplied.

Compare captured passages and confirm the product, market, family and version. An observed mention does not establish the first approved addition. Exports are informational review packages, not regulatory submissions. Review the current disclaimer and original evidence before reliance.
