# Country Explorer

## Select a jurisdiction

Open **Explore → Country Explorer**. Choose a country or jurisdiction and select Explore. The current product repository supports US, Canadian and EU source records. For EU member countries, the product view shows EU centrally evaluated evidence; it does not establish national availability. Other countries retain regulator links and available safety updates, with the national registry marked as not connected.

## Read products and company roles

Search by product, ingredient, source company name or application identifier. Search ignores case, accepts spaced FDA application identifiers and allows words in different orders. Filter by source category and status group. Counts are source records, with possible overlap between registries; they are not counts of unique marketed medicines.

An NDC listing is not FDA approval. A source authorisation does not establish that a medicine is marketed. Source company roles are not proof of global commercial ownership. Open official evidence links to verify each record. US application records can link into the US Approval & Label Journey.

## Review and export evidence

The Companies view groups source company names within the current product filters. Safety updates search captured country updates independently of the product filters. The Clinical activity tab retrieves country-located ClinicalTrials.gov interventional drug and biological studies on demand. Filter loaded studies by phase, overall study status and search terms. Load subsequent registry pages explicitly. The source query total includes records excluded from this medicine-only view. Overall status is not local site recruitment status, and study medicines may include comparators and placebo. A country switch clears clinical pages and continuation tokens. Failed requests preserve previously loaded evidence for retry. A missing or unavailable snapshot is a coverage gap, not evidence that no records exist.

Export downloads the filtered loaded product and update records with coverage information and limitations. It contains no live verification of national completeness. Review source dates before using a downloaded file; exports can become stale.
