# Safety Topic Explorer

## Choose a topic

Open **Explore → Safety Topics**. No sign-in is needed. Search the topic catalog by topic name or phrase, then choose a topic. The URL preserves your topic and evidence filters for sharing. This is an editorial discovery catalog, not a licensed dictionary or validated safety query.

## Review the evidence timeline

Filter by evidence type, agency or source, and publication date. Dated items appear newest first; unknown dates appear last and are excluded by a publication-date filter. Reset filters to restore the whole topic view. Show more evidence loads the next 30 items.

Open **Why this matched** to see the phrase, matching field and excerpt. A match can be a background mention or a negative finding. Read the original source before interpreting it. Regulator communications and secondary reports are identified separately. FDA approved-change excerpts and EMA recommendation indexes retain their different status. Research cards show available correction/retraction notices; inspect paper details and the original publication before use. The excerpt is normalized for matching rather than a replacement for the original document. Each evidence type remains identified; research does not become a regulator requirement.

## Continue into company review

Label items open the existing Label Intelligence record, where source review and confirmed company products are required before a private assessment. Research opens the paper detail page. Updates link to Company Briefing so you can review your confirmed portfolio and matched evidence before assessment. These links do not create assessments automatically. Use the existing Company Inbox and Action Center to decide applicability, assign actions and verify closure.

Ingredients shown on a label card are names listed by that source. They are not inferred causal relationships. Use Substance Explorer to inspect product and authorization-holder evidence.

## Coverage and interpretation

The explorer uses PV Info’s collected updates, research metadata and captured label sections. It does not download every full paper or every historical label. Unavailable collections produce an explicit incomplete-results message; no matches does not prove no risk. Publication dates do not represent effective dates or when a company first learned of an issue.

The catalog and phrase rules are versioned with application releases. It is not MedDRA, an SMQ, WHODrug, a signal detection algorithm or an automatic legal interpretation. No patient-level information is collected by this module.
