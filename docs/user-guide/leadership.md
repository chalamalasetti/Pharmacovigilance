# Leadership Cockpit and Company Safety Watch

## Open your company workspace

Use **My Briefing → Leadership Cockpit**, or follow the link from Company Change Inbox. Sign-in and approval are required for private company work. Public explorers remain available without approval. Confirm products and markets in Company Briefing first; changed product evidence requires reconfirmation before the cockpit loads.

Choose a confirmed company and My private account or an authorized team. Market filters narrow the loaded company evidence and case context. Team cases remain subject to existing membership permissions. Your topic/function preferences remain personal even when viewing a team; they are not shared team configuration.

## Configure my watch

Select safety topics and functional review interests, then Save my safety watch. Products and markets come from the confirmed portfolio, not a duplicate product list. Topic phrases match only evidence already scoped to that portfolio or the applicable jurisdiction watch. Available original update summaries and research abstracts enrich existing matches without adding unrelated medicines or changing case identities. When these are unavailable, the title and captured match context are used. Negative findings and background mentions can match; inspect the excerpt. Functions are suggested review interests, not assignments or assumptions about installed Argus, Veeva or ARISg configurations.

Watch preferences are stored privately in the database. A server revision prevents a stale tab from overwriting a newer save. On conflict, keep your selections, reload and compare before retrying. With no selected topics the cockpit still shows portfolio evidence and open work; it does not claim all risks are covered.

## Read daily priorities

The suggested order is overdue assigned actions, pending verification, actions due within seven days, other open work, then unverified upcoming source-date prompts, watched-topic matches and remaining portfolio evidence. This is a transparent work-order suggestion, not an approved risk score. Open Why this needs attention for match reasons, topic excerpts, functional prompts and original date wording.

Metrics count existing assessments/actions in the selected scope. Evidence without a linked assessment is shown separately. Actions due within seven days excludes overdue work and later due dates. More information needed reflects recorded applicability decisions; the app does not invent blockers or current-control gaps. Click a metric to filter the priority list.

Source-date prompts require official/source evidence and a valid day-specific date within 30 days. Month-only, invalid or research/secondary-report dates are not promoted into deadlines. Extracted source dates are unverified and do not establish a company implementation obligation. Publication date is separate from effective date and first detection.

## Continue the same review journey

Existing work opens the original Action Center case. New product/research evidence opens its exact item in Company Change Inbox, preserving company and authorized team scope; use the existing assessment action there. Label and regulatory items retain their required source-review routes. No case is created automatically by the cockpit.

The Inbox can open focused evidence even if you have already marked it seen. Reset filters removes that focus. Reading markers remain private to you and separate from team assessment state. Changed evidence with a closed case can link back to that case for comparison; it does not automatically reopen the case or replace pinned evidence. Label source-version changes keep the existing version-specific identities.

## Weekly leadership brief

The last seven calendar dates show recorded applicability decisions, closure-approval events and evidence publication dates, alongside current open-work counts. Case-event dates use your browser time zone. A closure count does not certify independent review, and reopened cases retain their history. Current open counts are not reconstructed historical end-of-week balances.

Download private weekly brief saves a text snapshot with company, market/privacy scope, priorities, ownership/due dates where recorded, decision/closure links and coverage limitations. The export uses the selected company/market/privacy scope, not the priority-view filter. It is not automatically sent to anyone. Share it only with authorized recipients. The downloadable priority list is capped at 20; use the app and original case records for the full queue and evidence.

## Coverage and recovery

The cockpit uses available product/research matches within the existing company evidence window, captured label evidence and the latest regulatory source review per document. Clinical-study changes remain in Inbox and Portfolio 360. Missing coverage is not an absence of obligations. Technical feed diagnostics remain admin-only; the cockpit explains decision-relevant coverage limitations.

If assessment status, watch preferences or reading history cannot load completely, the cockpit blocks its counts rather than presenting a false complete result. A regulatory/label collection failure is disclosed as partial coverage. Reload after changes elsewhere; this release is a snapshot view, not a push notification service. Same-account token refresh retains the watch form; sign-out/account changes clear private content. Existing company decisions, actions and closure records are never rewritten by viewing or saving a watch.
