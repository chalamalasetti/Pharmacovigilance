# Social Post Studio

## Who can use it

Open **Admin → Social Post Studio**. The workspace checks the signed-in account's administrator status before loading updates or showing editorial controls. Readers and other non-admin accounts see a sign-in/access message. Professional responsibility preferences never grant access.

## Choose an update

The current public update snapshot is ranked using source classification, recency, regulatory/safety topic, source-summary availability and keyword-based impact prompts. The top eligible item is selected automatically. Search and choose another update as needed. Invalid dates, future-dated items beyond a one-day tolerance and unsafe links are excluded. Duplicate source links are collapsed.

Ranking is an editorial suggestion, not clinical risk or legal significance. A source tagged official has not thereby been independently verified. Open the source and check its current status, dates, applicability and primary evidence. Older items and secondary sources carry explicit warnings in the selection rationale.

## Review the post and image

The template produces an editable LinkedIn draft with a short attributed source excerpt, potential business and systems/data impacts, the source link and a PV Info introduction. No paid AI service is used. Keyword prompts are not comprehensive document analysis or legal advice.

Preview the post and square visual card. The image is a 1,200 × 1,200 PNG with branding and two potential-impact prompts. Edit those prompts separately using **Edit image impact prompts**. Editing the post does not change the image. Longer graphic text is shortened to fit; include the accompanying source-linked text when sharing.

## Export and publish

Check all three editorial statements after reviewing the source, both outputs, attribution and privacy. Editing either output resets these checks. Keep the LinkedIn post within the displayed 3,000-character limit. Then copy the post, download its text or download the PNG. Clipboard failures have a text-download fallback.

These checks allow export; they do not create a regulatory approval record. Publish manually from the organization's chosen social account. PV Info does not connect to LinkedIn or send the post for you.

## Draft storage and access

Drafts remain only in the current page's memory. They are not stored in a public database, local storage or GitHub. Download before reloading or leaving. Switching tabs hides the workspace until administrator access is checked again, then restores the draft for the same account. Sign-out or account changes clear it. Do not enter personal data or private company information into promotional content.
