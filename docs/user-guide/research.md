# Research, papers and innovation

Find papers, follow ideas over time and assess research without treating exploratory findings as established practice.

## Search the research collection
Open [Research](https://www.pvinfo.org/research.html). Search terms can name an application, method, medicine or safety topic. Combine PV application, Method or technology, Publication type, Evidence maturity and Published within. Open access only and New since my last visit further narrow the collection. Clear filters resets the view.

Use Latest research for the current list, Innovation timeline for the available chronology, and My watchlist for saved research preferences. Show more loads additional matching records. The collection reflects configured sources and collection cadence; it is not a systematic literature review or complete worldwide corpus.

## Read a paper
Open a paper's detail page. Check title, authorship/publication metadata where provided, venue, publication date, identifier, abstract/summary and original publication links. Check whether it is a preprint, peer-reviewed paper, review or other indexed type. Open the original publication to verify methods, limitations and version status.

Evidence-maturity and relevance labels combine app rules and available editorial review. They are decision aids, not a guarantee of clinical validity, regulatory acceptance, production readiness or superiority to existing practice. Topic tags and summaries can be incomplete or wrong.

## Use the innovation timeline well
Inspect how papers/ideas appear over time and compare their application and maturity. An increase in indexed publications may reflect source coverage or tagging changes, not an industry-wide adoption trend. A date on the timeline is normally publication evidence, not the date a method was adopted in production.

## Save and assess research
Use the supported watchlist/save controls to revisit a paper. A confirmed company briefing can match research by product/substance context. Send for assessment where available, then use Action Center to record whether a finding warrants literature follow-up, a process evaluation or another action. A paper should not become a mandatory legal requirement merely because it matches a company ingredient.

## Administrator: review research metadata
Open [Research review](https://www.pvinfo.org/research-admin.html) with an authorized account. Select the relevant review queue and expand Review for a paper. Check automatic topic tags, relevance/maturity classifications, summary status and links between versions of the same paper. Mark topics as checked only after review, then Save review.

Clear review removes the supported editorial overlay; it does not retract or change the original paper. History exposes prior review events. Review changes may await the next data publication before appearing in the public research snapshot. Do not include private company conclusions or personal contact details in public editorial content.
