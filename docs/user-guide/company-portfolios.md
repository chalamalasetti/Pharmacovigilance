# Company Explorer and Portfolio 360

Explore marketed products, study medicines, therapeutic areas and source coverage before confirming a private company scope.

## Find the correct company
Open [Company Explorer](https://www.pvinfo.org/companies.html). Search by company name, alias, brand or substance; narrow the available browse/type filters. Check legal names, former names, parent/subsidiary information and disambiguation notes before choosing a result. Similar company names are not necessarily the same legal entity.

Following a company is a reading preference. Visiting a public company page does not publish your private selected portfolio or reveal which other users follow it.

## Use the company tabs
| Tab | What it shows and how to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Profile, evidence coverage and summary context. Read provisional/draft notices. |
| Portfolio 360 | Public clinical-study evidence, conditions/therapeutic groupings, study phases and medicines. |
| Related updates | Updates matched by company/product/substance/class context. Inspect why each match was made. |
| Products & markets | Source-linked product records, market, authorization number, holder, ingredient and status. |
| Research | Related papers with maturity and match context. |
| Sources & coverage | Source provenance, last good collection and explicit coverage gaps. |

## Explore marketed products
Use Search products for a brand, substance or authorization number. Filter the available markets and statuses. A discontinued or withdrawn source record is not the same as a currently marketed medicine. Holder names and company mappings can require review, especially after mergers or licensing changes.

Current product mapping relies principally on the configured FDA Orange Book and EMA medicines sources. Do not infer complete worldwide product coverage, all US biologics/vaccines, every national authorization or all licensing relationships. Missing products may reflect a coverage or mapping gap rather than true absence.

## Find clinical trials and investigational medicines
1. Open Portfolio 360.
2. Search by NCT ID, study medicine or condition.
3. Combine Trial phase, Trial status, Therapeutic area / condition group, Study country and Company trial relationship filters. More filters exposes additional options when collapsed.
4. Distinguish Lead sponsor from Collaborator. The same company can have different reported roles across studies.
5. Inspect study medicines and experimental-arm evidence. Open the official trial record to verify status, dates, intervention details and organization relationships.
6. Load more registry pages when available. Reset clinical filters if a loaded subset has no matches.

Therapeutic areas are groupings from condition evidence. They are not a medically validated disease ontology or proof of a company's commercial franchise. Study medicines do not establish ownership, approval, active development rights or inclusion in a company's marketed portfolio. Phases may overlap in study records, so phase counts are not a count of unique products.

## Read collection indicators
Complete, partial, not checked, source unavailable and mapping-outdated states describe collection/mapping evidence. A partial result can preserve the prior usable portfolio while a refresh is unfinished. Review retrieval dates and source status before relying on a study's current status. No loaded results is not a statement that the company has no studies.

## Prepare a company briefing
Select Build my company briefing to confirm the company, product records and markets in the next workflow. You still make the scope decision; exploring Portfolio 360 does not automatically enroll every study medicine in your confirmed marketed-product profile.

Missing a company? Use the organizational request form in Company Explorer, with a public company website where available. Do not add individual contacts or confidential pipeline information.
