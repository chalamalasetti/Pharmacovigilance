# Confirm My Company Briefing

Select a company, confirm products and markets, then hand relevant evidence to assessment.

## Before you start
Use an approved account to save a private company profile. Public company evidence remains browseable without approval. Decide whether you are setting up a personal workspace or a selected team's work. Use real organizational scope only where you are authorized to maintain it.

## Confirm products and markets
1. Open [My Company Briefing](https://www.pvinfo.org/company-briefing.html).
2. Search Company name or alias and choose the correct result.
3. Read the company's coverage and product-mapping limitations.
4. Select the markets you need to monitor.
5. Select the supported product records relevant to that scope. Verify source, ingredient and authorization identifiers rather than relying only on brand text.
6. Select Confirm scope and build briefing.
7. Check that the displayed company, market and product selections match your intention.

A confirmed profile records your selection and its revision/confirmation context. It is not a declaration that public source coverage is complete. The current product selection is based on the records the app can verify; do not assume unlisted investigational medicines are automatically covered.

## Read the matched briefing
Updates to review and Research to explore are organized around your confirmed scope. Read the source, publication date, match reason and affected market. A direct product match is more specific than a substance, class, corporate or jurisdiction association, but none replaces an applicability decision.

Review outside-market notices deliberately. A foreign source can be useful for signal monitoring without imposing the same local legal obligation. The app may require an explicit outside-market confirmation before an assessment handoff.

## Send an item for assessment
1. Open its source evidence and review why it matched.
2. Select Send for assessment where available.
3. Confirm the intended work scope/destination.
4. Read the saved/under-assessment confirmation.
5. Continue in [Action Center](action-center.md); a briefing handoff is not a completed applicability decision or assigned action.

An existing handoff should be continued rather than recreated. Reading, saving, starting assessment and verified closure are different states. The [Company Change Inbox](company-change-inbox.md) helps you keep those states separate.

## Keep the scope current
When source product records or mappings change, the app can require reconfirmation before new assessments. Reopen Company Briefing, inspect the changed portfolio, reselect/confirm the intended products and markets, then reload the inbox. Existing cases retain their pinned evidence and portfolio context; a new public product does not silently rewrite an old case.

Automated source refresh can add or update public products/studies. It does not mean every new medicine is automatically selected into your confirmed private profile, or that a new study has been classified as company-applicable.

## Privacy
Many users can browse J&J, GSK, AstraZeneca or any other public company without exposing their personal selection to one another. Public company pages are shared reference evidence. A personal profile, reading history and company case remain within the applicable account/team controls. Team work is intentionally shared with authorized members. Review the chosen scope before exporting or sharing.
