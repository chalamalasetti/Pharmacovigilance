# Regulatory Impact Briefs

## Find a brief
Open **Resources → Safety resources → Regulatory Impact Briefs**, or follow the link in Configuration Workbench, the EMA guidance example, matching source documents or exact-source Impact cards. This release contains two curated worked examples: FDA expedited IND and EMA outbound ICSR masking. It does not automatically generate analysis for arbitrary newly ingested guidance.

## Review evidence before deciding
Choose a brief and reading role: Configuration manager, Safety scientist or PV/regulatory lead. The source bundle separates paraphrased source observations from proposed implementation controls and unresolved company questions. Check exact source locations and originals. Source links can change; the FDA HTML pages are not archived snapshots in these briefs. The EMA detail retains its supported source checksum and field-level recipe.

Both briefs are explicitly **unreviewed drafts**, not approved legal baselines or premium reviewed publications. A package hash verifies the brief's content identity, not its scientific or legal correctness. No public editorial approval has been recorded. The current release has no public brief approval/publishing editor.

## Interpret dates and unknowns
Date panels distinguish historical cutoffs, anticipated agency milestones and event-triggered clocks. These are not automatically approved calendar records. FDA closed applications must not be treated as an invitation to apply. A forecast is not a compliance deadline; a pre-IND upload does not establish the final IND receipt event. Open Regulatory Calendar for its separate verified-date workflow.

Company current controls, applicability and gaps remain unknown until assessed. A justified no-change decision is valid. Nothing here establishes a new expedited safety-reporting rule merely because a page mentions FDA, IND or ESG.

## Prepare private work
Answer the fixed screening questions, choose your safety system and select **Prepare private assessment**. Answers stay in memory until handoff, then pass through same-tab session storage into Configuration Workbench; no company name, patient data or contact details are requested here. Approved sign-in is required to save. Workbench clears the handoff after loading it. Do not use a shared browser session for private company work.

The draft preserves brief ID, version and hash; company and installed release remain blank, source verification is unchecked, baseline incomplete and applicability needs more information. Confirm those facts, inspect current controls, record the decision and use existing workbench revisions, team review, export and Action Center handoff. Saved private work is governed by existing account/team access. No live vendor settings are changed and no tests are claimed executed. Editing the source identity clears its brief provenance pin.

## Export and limitations
**Export pinned brief** downloads the public, unreviewed JSON package. Company work is exported separately through the workbench. Keep the exact version when reviewing changes; an unavailable requested hash is rejected rather than silently replaced. This is a curated evidence-to-decision pilot without a paid model API, autonomous legal interpretation, installed-system inspection or automatic regulatory publication.

## My Company Impact Assessment
New brief handoffs include **5 · Company impact** in Configuration Workbench. Choose an existing confirmed company portfolio, or enter the company environment in step 1. A portfolio copies its company, markets and revision reference; it does not verify current product availability or applicability. Enter the program/products, development stage and operating responsibilities, then confirm the scope.

For every source-linked proposal, capture the actual current control and an organizational evidence reference. Choose **Supported**, **Potential gap**, **Unknown** or **Not applicable** and record a rationale. Potential gaps require an action. These expectations are implementation inferences from the unreviewed brief, not automatically approved legal requirements. Do not include patient data, names, contact details, credentials or case exports.

Record an overall decision: change required, no change required, more information needed, or not applicable. No-change and not-applicable decisions cannot retain unknowns or potential gaps. A not-applicable decision requires every row to be not applicable. Change-required decisions need a gap and action. Supported/gap rows need current-control evidence. Server-side validation enforces these gates on submission/approval, as well as existing source verification, baseline and review requirements. A no-change decision cannot accompany a reporting-rule modification.

Use **Review source and applicability** to complete step 3; the overall decision does not silently set source verification or applicability. A not-applicable overall decision may use Not relevant applicability with its rationale. All other approvals retain the existing relevant-applicability gate. Save drafts while researching unknowns. Generate & compare shows the control comparison; JSON export and revision snapshots preserve the full data. Existing team approval requires a different team owner. Use the existing Action Center handoff to assign actions and verify closure against the saved configuration revision.

Older saved work remains readable and is not retroactively populated or reapproved. Start a new draft from a brief to use the company comparison. The module does not inspect installed safety systems, execute tests or publish company assessments publicly.
