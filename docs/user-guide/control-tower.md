# PV Control Tower

## Open your company view

Open **My Briefing → PV Control Tower**, or use the Control Tower link on My Briefing, Company Briefing or Action Center. First confirm products and markets in [Company Briefing](company-briefing.md). Each company has its own monitoring scope.

Select a company and a work visibility scope. **My private account**, **Team**, and **This browser only** are separate views. Account work uses existing access controls; selecting a company does not grant access to other users' cases. Browser-only work is visible to anyone using the same browser profile. Pending accounts need approval for company work.

## Review what needs attention

The summary cards open filtered priority lists: overdue actions, actions due within seven days, awaiting assessment, awaiting verification, and evidence without a linked case in the selected scope. Priorities show recorded owners, assigned due dates and links to original evidence and existing cases.

Work comes from [Action Center](action-center.md). Evidence candidates come from confirmed-product company update and research matches over the last 365 days and captured label changes. Missing dates can appear and do not mean that evidence is new. A source match is not a decision on applicability. Existing cases, including closed cases, suppress duplicate new-case prompts; this does not establish that a later source revision has been assessed. Review changed evidence through [Company Inbox](company-change-inbox.md).

## Focus products and markets

Use the market and product filters or **Focus product** in Products × markets. Product counts can overlap because an item can concern multiple products. Case context is the frozen product scope recorded when the assessment began; it can differ from today's portfolio. Company-wide cases without product details appear in the all-product view.

Review areas are keyword prompts for evidence without a linked case. They are not assignments, responsibility determinations or notifications. Assigned action dates are internal recorded dates, not automatically calculated statutory reporting deadlines.

## Continue the work

**Continue in Action Center** opens the existing case in its original scope. In browser-only mode, new evidence opens Company Briefing for a local assessment handoff. Signed-in private and team evidence opens Company Inbox; label evidence opens Label Intelligence. No new cases, assignments or decisions are created automatically.

Use Refresh after another tab changes work or portfolio scope. Reconfirm changed product records before loading new priorities. Signing out, changing accounts or losing approval clears the current private dashboard. Do not rely on a downloaded snapshot after access or source evidence changes.

## Download and interpret a snapshot

**Download management snapshot** saves a JSON file for the current company, market, product and visibility scope. It includes loaded-at time, portfolio revision, priorities, product counts, recorded dates and explicit coverage limitations. Keep private downloads under your company's document controls.

If cases cannot load completely, work metrics and evidence-to-case linkage are unavailable, not zero. If labels cannot load, other evidence remains available with a coverage gap. The coverage panel separates company confirmation, source capture dates and the current dashboard load time.

The Control Tower is not a compliance attestation or a validated regulatory record. It does not automatically aggregate every country requirement, clinical-study change, product licence, partner agreement or statutory deadline. [Leadership Cockpit](leadership.md) offers topic watches and a weekly brief; Company Inbox includes additional regulatory and clinical evidence. Qualified human review is mandatory.
