# My Company Change Inbox

Triage new evidence and continue company work without confusing reading status with assessment status.

## Open your company inbox
1. Confirm a company, products and markets in [Company Briefing](company-briefing.md).
2. Open [My Company Change Inbox](https://www.pvinfo.org/company-inbox.html).
3. Choose Confirmed company and Work privacy scope.
4. Select Reload inbox after a portfolio change, source refresh or action taken elsewhere.

If the screen asks you to reconfirm a changed portfolio, return to Company Briefing first. Do not assume the app's previous product selection still represents the latest source records.

## Filter the evidence queue
Search evidence narrows available items. Evidence type separates product updates, research, regulatory source reviews, clinical studies and label changes. Reading state offers Unread & updated, All reading states, Seen and Updated since seen. Reset filters returns to the default view and Show next 30 reveals further items.

The evidence queue contains what matched available company context. It is not an exhaustive list of every publication or every company obligation. Clinical evidence and regulator-wide reviews may have broader matching logic than a directly identified product.

## Read, mark and assess
| Control/state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Mark as seen | Records that you reviewed this evidence version in the current scope. It does not approve applicability. |
| Mark unread | Returns it to your reading queue; it does not reopen a closed case. |
| Updated since seen | The evidence fingerprint changed after the recorded reading marker. Recheck it. |
| Start assessment | Opens the appropriate company assessment when evidence and permissions are ready. |
| Continue assessment | Opens the existing linked case rather than creating parallel work. |
| View verified closure | Opens the existing completed case; inspect its decision/evidence rather than inferring a new assessment. |
| Review company trials | Takes you to clinical evidence. A study row is not automatically a regulatory action case. |

For a regulatory source review awaiting human triage, Start assessment is locked or replaced by a source-review route. Use Regulatory Change Review to compare and triage it first. Matching alone must not turn an unconfirmed text change into a company obligation.

## How Inbox and Action Center work together
Inbox answers: what is new, what have I read, and is there existing work? Action Center answers: does it apply, what action is assigned, and has closure been verified? Starting a case from Inbox carries the original evidence and company context forward. Opening the same item again should take you to that case, not create another decision trail.

Your open work summarizes awaiting assessment, actions due, overdue and awaiting verification for the selected company/scope. Switch to the intended personal account or team before interpreting those counts.

## Troubleshooting
If reading status cannot be saved, retry before assuming the marker persisted. If assessment status cannot load, do not create a duplicate to bypass the error. Reload the inbox, check sign-in/approval, then continue the existing case. If no items appear, clear the reading-state filter and verify both the company scope and source coverage.

## Label and topic discovery

Matched label records use **Review label evidence** before assessment. Complete the official-source review in [Label Intelligence](label-intelligence.md), verify the confirmed company products and privacy scope, then open or continue the pinned case. An EMA recommendation index does not prove that local wording has changed.

[Safety Topic Explorer](safety-topics.md) is a public discovery view, not another private inbox. Opening a topic does not mark company evidence as seen, decide applicability or create a case. [Substance Explorer](substances.md) provides public ingredient/product evidence; your private scope still requires Company Briefing confirmation.
