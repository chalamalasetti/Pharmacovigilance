# Start here

Understand what PV Info does, choose your starting point and complete a useful first session.

## What PV Info helps you do
PV Info brings public pharmacovigilance updates, research, company product evidence and regulatory source documents into a shared reading and review environment. It also supports private company assessments, assigned actions and recorded closure. An automatically matched item is a review prompt; it is not a conclusion that a requirement applies to your company.

## Your first ten minutes
1. Open [Updates](https://www.pvinfo.org/) and select a country or body, a topic and a publication window.
2. Open an update's original source. Check its publication date, jurisdiction and authority before relying on its summary.
3. Open [Company Explorer](https://www.pvinfo.org/companies.html) and find a company, brand or substance. Inspect Sources & coverage so you understand the limits of the portfolio.
4. Explore [Substances](https://www.pvinfo.org/substances.html) for ingredient/product relationships, [Label Intelligence](https://www.pvinfo.org/labels.html) for captured wording changes, or [Safety Topics](https://www.pvinfo.org/safety-topics.html) for cross-source evidence. Open [Research](https://www.pvinfo.org/research.html) to explore papers and the innovation timeline, or [Source Documents](https://www.pvinfo.org/documents.html) to inspect regulatory evidence.
5. If you need a saved company workspace, [create an account](https://www.pvinfo.org/request-access.html), choose a user ID and password, and check your approval status. Public browsing remains available while approval is pending.
6. After approval, configure [My Company Briefing](https://www.pvinfo.org/company-briefing.html), confirm products and markets, then use the [Company Change Inbox](https://www.pvinfo.org/company-inbox.html) and [Action Center](https://www.pvinfo.org/action-center.html).

## Understand access before saving
| Access state | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Not signed in | Public reading and discovery. Some existing tools offer browser-only workspace features. These are not a private cloud account or a shared team record. |
| Signed in, approval pending | Public modules remain accessible. View private approval status and administrator messages. Personal company profiles, assessment saves and team work remain locked until approval. |
| Approved account | Save supported personal work and use authorized team workflows. Select the intended privacy scope before saving. |
| Site administrator | Additional account, source, content and refresh controls; access is checked by the server. A professional-role preference does not grant administrator rights. |

## Navigation and guide conventions
Menus are grouped by default into Updates, My Briefing, Explore and Resources. Administrators can rename groups, move items and hide optional links. The page names and direct links in this guide remain useful when your menu labels differ. Controls that depend on a selected record may appear only after you select it. A hidden menu entry does not replace access control.

This guide describes the implemented application, not proposed backlog features. It was reviewed against the Drug Name Resolver release, including Substance Explorer, Label Intelligence and Safety Topic Explorer on 3 November 2026. Actual data coverage and review status are shown inside the app. The guide is not evidence that the application is validated for a regulated intended use.

## Keep work appropriate for this app
Use organizational descriptions. Do not enter patient narratives, dates of birth, personal addresses, phone/fax numbers or credentials into searches, public annotations, requirement text, evidence links or support messages. Where a workflow explicitly requests an owner/reviewer email, enter only the authorized work identifier. Never publish passwords, recovery keys, temporary passwords or private assessment exports in GitHub issues.
