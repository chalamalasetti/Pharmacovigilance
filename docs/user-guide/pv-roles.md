# PV Roles, Processes & My Responsibilities

## Open the operating model
Open **Safety resources → PV Roles & Processes**, the Account page link, or **My Briefing → My responsibility briefing**. Public readers can explore the reference directory and use a temporary selection. The first catalog contains 63 reference roles across 18 functions and 30 processes. Job titles can be combined or outsourced. This is not a staffing standard, qualification requirement, legal appointment or permissions directory.

Search a role or responsibility and filter by function/process. Each card explains its reference responsibility and associated processes. The legal-role note and primary references explain why statutory roles need jurisdiction-specific review. No name, employer appointment, CV, degree, licence number or contact details are requested.

## Save my responsibilities
Select multiple role cards and optional processes, then **Save my responsibilities**. Approved sign-in is required for private database persistence. Preferences contain catalog IDs, not professional credentials; choosing QPPV, auditor or system administrator grants no additional access or approval rights. Other users and anonymous readers cannot read your saved preferences. Unsaved selections are temporary. A stale save is rejected: reload before changing preferences in another session. Retired roles remain traceable and can be removed from a prior selection.

## Build a responsibility briefing
Choose roles/processes, then **Build responsibility briefing**. The app screens up to the 2,000 most recent updates in the current public data snapshot and shows up to 20 matched updates. Each association explains the matched process terms and suggested role. This is deterministic keyword screening, not autonomous regulatory interpretation or a complete monitoring service. No match does not mean no impact. Selection is applied in the browser; it is not sent to source agencies. Existing country/topic briefing and feed refresh behavior remain separate.

Impact cards also offer **Responsibilities & processes to consider**, with up to 12 screening associations. These generic card suggestions do not use your private preferences. Review the source before drawing an applicability conclusion.

## Review roles in a Regulatory Impact Brief
The **Role & process responsibility map** shows source-linked associations for the two curated briefs. If private role/process preferences are available, they filter this map; otherwise all available associations are shown. Every association identifies role, process, suggested accountable/responsible/consulted/informed relationship, rationale, source location, proposed deliverable, revision and review state.

Initial associations are **suggested**, not human-reviewed. A reviewed association means an administrator recorded an editorial mapping decision; it is not legal approval, a company appointment, or permission to execute work. No mapped role for a selection does not establish no impact. Unsupported updates do not automatically receive a reviewed brief.

## Allocate company ownership
A new private Company Impact Assessment offers optional accountable, responsible, consulted and informed **reference-role IDs** for each control. Company users confirm actual allocation; public mapping suggestions never silently assign ownership. These fields do not contain people or grant app access. They stay inside existing private/team package revisions, JSON exports and print output, with the assessment's decisions and evidence. Existing independent-review and Action Center controls still apply. Role reference changes do not automatically rewrite saved company records; reassess the organization's operating model explicitly.

## Admin catalog and mapping review
Use **Admin → Manage role catalog & responsibility mappings**, or open the role page and its admin section. Only verified administrators see the editor; the database enforces authorization independently.

- Add a stable reference role ID, title, responsibility, existing function, process associations and change rationale.
- Edit or retire a role; do not recycle an ID for a materially different responsibility. Function/process definitions and screening terms are release-controlled in this first version.
- Add a brief/role/process association with original-source URL, exact location, rationale and proposed work product. New associations start suggested.
- Review the source and choose suggested, reviewed or retired, with the relationship and rationale. Review dates are recorded only for reviewed decisions.
- Editing a role reference returns its reviewed associations to suggested for reassessment. Retired mappings stop appearing in public brief maps.
- Load editorial history to inspect changes and reasons. Ordinary users, including administrators, cannot overwrite the stored audit rows through the app.

A stale edit is rejected. Role-model management does not modify authentication roles or baseline legal obligations. The initial catalog is a controlled reference model, not a claim of exhaustive global requirements.
