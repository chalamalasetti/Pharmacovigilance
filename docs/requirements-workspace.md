# Requirements workspace

The requirements page now hosts four product views: explorer, research intake, coverage and company workflow. The prior baseline URL redirects to research intake. No Markdown documents are served by this feature.

The explorer retains the existing immutable requirement versions, editorial review, comparison, date filtering and private/team assessment handoff. Research candidates remain a separate static pending dataset; this release does not promote them into approved database versions. Coverage reports captured interpretations and research counts, never regulatory completeness. Offline review counts are unavailable rather than inferred.

The new stylesheet is scoped to the requirements workspace to preserve the homepage and source document layouts. Existing browser journeys now cover workspace switching, research citations, Japan gaps, company action links and mobile overflow in addition to review and private handoff.
